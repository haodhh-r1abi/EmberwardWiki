1HOLYSacred FlameUnlocked after completing the Misty Forest on normal difficulty.Able to sense the slightest corruption, all battle stages in the adventure are corrupted stages, with stronger enemies and greater rewards. When a corrupted tile appears within the Sacred Flame's attack range, it will prioritize attacking that tile to cleanse the corruption. At the start of each turn, the Sacred Flame's attack range increases by 2 tiles (up to +20 tiles).
2BLAZEArcflare FlameUnlocked after completing the Bone Desert on normal difficulty.The Arcflare Flame periodically releases bursts of electrical energy, shocking random enemies on the field. Hit monsters are stunned for 1–2 seconds, and their [CHARGE] is increased by 20%. When enemies enter its attack range, it releases an electric current that strikes up to three targets simultaneously.
3DEMONSoul FlameUnlocked after completing the Frost Ruins on normal difficulty.When the Ember Spark talent kills enough monsters, it levels up and gains +1 attack damage. When damaged by monsters, the flame's level decreases by the amount of damage taken.
4ANCIENTAncient FlameUnlocked after completing the Sealed Citadel on normal difficulty.When placed, all your towers are sealed by corrupt energy, gaining +15% Critical Rate and +10% Attack Speed each round. This seal can last up to two rounds, but you may remove it at any time. At the end of each round, the flame releases corrupt energy, spreading corrupted tiles on the nearby ground.
5VOIDVoid FlameAt the start of each stage, gain two Void Portal cards, allowing you to place 2 pairs of portals on the field. Monsters that pass through a Void Portal are marked for 10 seconds and are treated as being within the Void Flame's attack range. The Void Flame's attacks corrode their targets, inflicting 20% [VULNERABLE] for 5 seconds. Does not affect Bosses.
6CANDLECandle FlameUnlocked after completing "Sealed Citadel" on heroic difficulty.A flickering candle flame with only 5 HP. The area around the fire source is shrouded in darkness, and your towers cannot attack monsters in the dark. Building towers expands your vision range. The larger the tower, the greater the vision it provides. When acquiring towers as rewards, you can choose twice.
7FROSTFrost FlameUnlocked after completing "Frost Ruins" on normal difficulty.Containing the essence from the Frost Ruins, Frost Flame continuously absorbs surrounding heat. The "Ember Spark" talent deals ice damage and inflicts chilling effect in a small area. At the end of each turn, the attack range of Frost Flame increases by 2 tiles, and freezes all blocks within the attack range.

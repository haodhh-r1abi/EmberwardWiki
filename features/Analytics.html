Mô tả

  • Unity Gaming Services Analytics (UnityServicesProjectConfiguration.json, Unity.Services.Core Telemetry): AnalyticsManager.Initialize → isAnalyticsEnabled; SendAnalytics(eventName, dict) log '[Analytics] Sent {0} with {1} fields'. Sự kiện xác định: level_finished {level_name, difficulty, is_success} (RecordLevelFinishedStatus), difficulty_selected {difficulty} (RecordDifficulty). Không có analytics tuỳ chỉnh khác; không remote config.
  • Awake: instance; Initialize (UnityServices.InitializeAsync → AnalyticsService.Instance.StartDataCollection — suy luận từ SDK). SendAnalytics gọi CustomData/RecordEvent.

Lưu ý

📌 Chưa xác định: Có bật consent/opt-out không — không thấy setting

Mở khóa & điểm vào

Điểm vào:

  • MainGame kết thúc stage → RecordLevelFinishedStatus
  • UI_DifficultySelection → RecordDifficulty

Cách hoạt động

  1. level_finished: RecordLevelFinishedStatus(levelName, difficulty, isSuccess) → SendAnalytics('level_finished', {level_name, difficulty, is_success})
  2. difficulty_selected: RecordDifficulty(diff) → SendAnalytics('difficulty_selected', {difficulty})
Luồng chi tiết theo code

Awake: instance; Initialize (UnityServices.InitializeAsync → AnalyticsService.Instance.StartDataCollection — suy luận từ SDK). SendAnalytics gọi CustomData/RecordEvent.
